# ISI-4 4.0.0: native permission decisions through shutdown

This major version strengthens V005 in
[ITCVE-1 2.0.0](../../../itcves/ITCVE-1/2.0.0/README.md).
The permanent eight vector identifiers and all 280 assertion identities remain.
V005 advances to version 4.0.0; V001–V004 retain 1.0.0, V006 retains 2.0.0,
and V007–V008 retain 3.0.0. The [catalog](catalog.json), explicit oracle
manifests and closure plan are fixed before a new execution.
[ISI-4 3.0.0](../3.0.0/README.md) and its original observations remain unchanged.

A passing native permission decision requires the actual trusted DOM click
on its matching visible, enabled control and a terminal public-wallet result
for the same pending-call nonce. Passive event capture precedes the wallet's
handler and survives popup closure. An automation target-closed error is retained;
it cannot substitute for the trusted event or the wallet's result. No permission
store, wallet callback or extension message is modified.

The explicit denied-payment boundary now continues through browser and service
shutdown. A complete final inventory of source and replacement provider ledgers
must preserve the original captured global prefix and every provider-local
suffix. Independent verification joins the immutable reports, native identity,
actual approved transaction bytes, cleanup ordering and worker receipt. No
matching denied or unapproved customer payment may appear through final capture;
only the separately proven approved transaction may use its declared exception.
Missing providers, incomplete cleanup and absent final evidence prevent admission.

The same 24 permission predicates cover protocol grants, basket access,
certificate disclosure, one-shot spending, visible individual and origin-wide
revocation, recovery and isolation. The strengthened capture and final ledger
are mandatory evidence for those criteria, not additional claims of supported
wallet features. Native ToDo lifecycle, storage replacement, the integrated graph
and clause corpus remain required under their separately frozen oracles.

Any implemented failure keeps its vector FAIL. V008 remains BLOCKED if only
unexecuted comprehensive obligations remain. Qualified evidence may demonstrate
reproducible failures and blockers; it is not complete BRC-100 conformance or
certification. The previous permission observer failures remain in their
original archives and cannot be promoted by this new evaluation.
